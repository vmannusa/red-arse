      How to play Red Arse

      Race all 4 of your marbles from your BASE, once around the board, and into your HOME column. First player with 4 marbles home wins.

      Your turn

      • Roll the die, then move one marble that many holes. Marbles travel clockwise.
      • Dotted arrows show every move you can make. Tap a marble, or tap a landing spot to move straight there.
      • You can jump over other players' marbles, but you can't jump over or land on your own.
      • If no move is possible, your turn ends.

      Getting out

      • Roll a 1 or a 6 to put a marble from your base on your start hole (the colored ring next to your base).

      Getting home

      • After a full lap, turn into your home column. You need the exact count and can't jump your own marbles there.

      Aggravate (kill)

      • Land exactly on an opponent to send that marble back to their base.
      • Moving backward or into the center can kill too.

      Roll again when you…

      • Roll a 6.
      • Get a kill.
      • End a move on an inside corner: the corner holes on either side of any home column. Your own start hole counts when you back onto it with a 4, not when a marble comes out of base.

      Shortcuts

      • Stars: a marble that starts its turn on a star can hop clockwise from star to star, one star per pip. The roll must be used exactly on stars (a 3 means exactly 3 stars), with no extra steps onto the track or into the center. You can't go past your own color's star.
      • Center hole: a roll that passes over a star with one step left can finish in the center. A 6 from your start hole goes straight in. Leave the center only on a 1, to any star.

      Backward 4

      • 4 is the only roll that can move backward: 4 holes counter-clockwise instead of forward.
      • If no forward move is possible on a 4, you must move backward.
      • Backward moves follow the track only. They can't hop stars or go straight into home, but they can drop into the center and kill.
      • Backing up behind your own start hole parks that marble right outside your home.

      Boards: the 6-player and 4-player hex boards play the same; the 4-player track is shorter (56 holes instead of 84). House rules can be switched off on the setup screen.
