# Red Arse – Change log

## 2026-10-16
- The 1 banner now reads **"Nice, One"**.
- **Partners mode** (Game settings, or the host in an online room; 4 or 6 players): partners sit opposite and win together when both homes are full. Once your 4 are home your rolls move your partner's marbles, and any roll with no move for your own marbles moves your partner's instead. Team tags in the player lists, team chance-to-win and review, a shared win screen, and bots that won't hit their partner unless forced.
- **Cougar paw marble:** the hand sign (thumb and ring finger touching), now the default for the Cougar Red theme.
- **Quick reactions:** 😂 😬 (OOF) 😡 🙄 under the die. They float up from the sender's base with a pop, show for everyone in online rooms, and have a 2-second cooldown per player.
- **Setup screen makeover:** Play online split into **Host a game** and **Join a room**; a second **Start game** button at the top of Players; **Shuffle seats**; bots with default names follow their color and taken colors swap seats; Rules shows "N of 12 active" and folds away after your first game; **See how** diagrams for inside corners, center hole, back-past-start and raids (also in How to play); the board theme previews live on the setup screen; the whole setup (including board size) is remembered, with **Reset defaults**.
- **Nice One** now shows now and then on a 1 (about 3 in 10) instead of every time; online, everyone sees the same callouts.
- "**X is sitting at home on the couch**" now takes turns at random with "Phil luck".
- **Nice One** banner on every roll of 1, and **COCKED** once or twice a game after a random roll.
- **Theme marbles:** picking a theme sets everyone's marble to match (Halloween pumpkins, Cougar Red basketballs, Christmas ornaments); players can still change their own. New **Pumpkin** and **Ornament** marbles.
- Each player's marble now shows in the turn panel, player list, stats and review, not just on the board.
- **Game settings** moved above Players on the setup screen.
- **Rolling crazy** and **Phil luck** now keep showing on every roll after the 3rd (every extra re-roll in the turn, every extra roll with no move), not just once.
- **Board themes:** Halloween (black and orange), Cougar Red (white and scarlet, HOUSTON along the bottom) and Christmas (evergreen with a candy-cane rim and Christmas-tree bases), each with its own **sound effects**. Pick one under Game settings; online, the host picks it in the room.
- **Sports-ball marbles, per player:** each player picks Glass, Golf, Basketball, Tennis or Baseball next to their color (setup screen and online rooms).
- **More banners:** "Back it up Terry" on a backward 4, "X is rolling crazy" on a 3rd re-roll in one turn, and "X had Phil luck" after 3 rolls in a row with no move. Banners now queue, so two in a row both show.
- **Move review fixes:** "Left your start hole blocked" no longer fires when you bring a marble out (it only counts if a marble was already parked there). New pattern: **Missed locking a home marble**. Every misstep and blunder now has a one-line reason under it.
- **Tested flexibility-minded playouts:** bots that value more marbles out didn't win more than normal bots over 6,000 games, so the review's playouts are unchanged.
- **Add to Home Screen** button, app icons and a web app manifest, so Red Arse can live on a phone's home screen and open full screen.
- **Link preview:** shared links now show a Red Arse picture, title and description instead of a gray box.

## 2026-10-15
- **Danger glow** on your marbles that can be hit next roll, a **kill banner** ("X aggravated Y"), **stuck-in-base** and **longest wait** stats with a "Most patient" award, **rivalries** (who aggravated whom, grudge matches), and a **Rematch** button (online: the host starts it, others can ask).
- **Black and White player colors:** pick them on the setup screen or in an online room. They take an open seat (bottom first), and the board, marbles and die all switch to black or white for that player.
- **Names on the board:** each player's name is engraved where their base used to say BASE, turned to read upright; the current player's name lights up in their color. Empty seats still say BASE.
- **Fix:** in online games, "Show on board" in the review could lose its Back button (the status line was refreshed when someone's connection changed). There's now a **Back to review** button on the board itself, and Esc works too. The review says "Played" instead of "You" so it reads right for every player's card.
- **Fix:** on some browsers one home ring (green's outermost slot) wasn't drawn, so green's home looked 3 long. The colored rings are now drawn without the etching filter and the filter covers the whole board. Every home was always 4 slots in the game itself.
- **Fairer move review:** close calls get more playouts and are graded on the cautious side, so 3-point coin flips no longer show as missteps; moves name where they go ("left the center to your home corner"); danger checks (review and Hard bots) now see inside-corner hop threats.
- **Claim your re-rolls:** after a 6, a kill or an outside corner, human players have 5 seconds to roll again with no reminder; miss it and the turn passes ("missed re-rolls" stat).
- **Simpler move review:** three grades (Good under 3 points, Misstep 3–10, Blunder 10+), every misstep and blunder listed, each named when it fits a pattern (missed a kill, a re-roll corner, the back-past-start shortcut, getting home, left your start hole blocked, left a marble in danger, and more).
- **Richer walnut and etched colors:** natural walnut grain with cathedral figure; the colored stars, arrows, bases and rings now look stained and carved into the wood; fancier glass marbles with a swirl inside.
- **New look:** dark walnut board (cream lettering and etched golfer, red logo), glass marbles with a highlight and glow, and a small countdown ring in the bottom-right corner of the board showing how long the current player has left.
- **Side panel rearranged:** the game feed and chat now sit right under the dice (where the player list was, since chance to win is on the board). Players & stats comes next with each player's marbles home and online status (you, away, offline), and the bot speed switch moved under it.
- **Game feed with chat:** the move log is now the game feed. In online games it has a chat bar and quick-chat buttons ("Back it up Terry" after a 4, "Nice 1" after a 1, COCKED, Nice roll, GG), an unread badge when it's collapsed, and it posts when a player goes away or comes back.
- **Backward inside-corner hop:** on a 4, a marble on an inside corner can hop exactly 4 inside corners counter-clockwise (6-player board), and going backward it can pass your own home corner.
- **Turn timer:** human players get 30 seconds (setup screen: Off, 15–90 seconds) to roll and again to move; when it runs out the game plays for them.
- **Game clock** in the turn panel and on the winner screen.
- **Bot speed:** Normal (a bit quicker than before), Fast or Turbo, on the setup screen and under the die during a game.
- **Official rules:** highest roll goes first (ties roll again); leave the center on a 1 or a 6; "outside corner" (edge hole, roll again) and "inside corner" (star, hop) now match the written rules.
- **Usage stats:** Google Analytics for Firebase (free) counts games started and finished, online rooms, players, bots and game length. No names are sent.
- **Online play is live:** Firebase project `red-arse` set up (anonymous sign-in, Realtime Database with room-only rules, vmannusa.github.io authorized) and connected in `config.js`. Choose **Play online** on the setup screen to create a room and share the invite link.
- **Move review:** every real choice a human player makes is graded Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake or Blunder by how much chance to win it gave up versus the best move. New stats: accuracy, blunders, mistakes, missed kills and points lost to moves (Acc also shows in the side panel; Corners moved to the full Stats window to make room).
- **End-of-game review:** a chance-to-win chart for the whole game, plus a card for each human player with their grades, whether the moves or the dice cost them, and their 3 costliest moves with the better play and a **Show on board** button.
- **Bots gang up on the leader:** as someone's chance to win climbs, Medium and Hard bots go after that player's marbles first; Hard bots also line up behind the leader's marbles.
- **What's new** link on the setup screen (next to How to play) and in the rules, pointing to this change log.
- Filled gaps in the in-game rules: a 1 from a star goes into the center, unlocked home marbles can back out on a 4, and how chance to win is worked out. Added RULES.md and this change log to GitHub.
- **Home rules:** home marbles are locked in only once they fill the column from the top with no gaps. Unlocked home marbles can move, and can back out of home on a 4 (forced when nothing else can move).
- **Raid a home:** turn into (or back into) another player's home to kill an unlocked marble; the raider stays there until it backs out on a 4.
- **Win chance on the board:** each player's percentage now sits in a pill beside their base.
- **Phones held sideways:** board sizes to the screen height with the controls beside it, so the whole board fits.
- **Star hops** can pass your own marbles on the stars in between (still can't land on one).
- **Star hops are exact:** one star per pip, the whole roll used on stars, no extra steps onto the track or into the center.
- **No jumping your own marbles** on the track, forward or backward.
- **Chance to win** for every player, updated after each move.
- **Etched golfer** added to the board-center logo.
- **Die color** matches whoever is rolling.
- **Backward 4** no longer hops stars.
- **Kill = roll again**, and a full **How to play** guide (also on the setup screen).
